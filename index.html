Australian Museum – Website Planning Document

This document explains the planning for a five‑page informational website about the Australian Museum. It includes the business details, sitemap, colour choices, wireframes, and all content that will appear on each page.

01 — Business

Business Name & Type

This website is based on the real Australian Museum in Sydney. All details such as address, phone number, and opening hours are accurate. The purpose of the website is to give information only.

CategoryDetails
Business NameAustralian Museum. Established in 1827. Australia’s first and oldest museum.

02 — Site Map

This sitemap shows the structure of the website. It has five main pages in the navigation bar: Home, About, Exhibitions, Visit Us, and Contact. The Exhibitions page has a filter that switches between All, Permanent, and Current exhibitions without reloading the page.

03 — Colour Scheme

This section shows the colours used in the website design. These colours were chosen because they are easy to read and match the museum theme. The table also shows the contrast ratios to make sure the text is accessible.

04 — Wireframes

This section shows the wireframes for all five pages of the website. Wireframes are simple drawings that show the layout of each page. They include image boxes, text areas, navigation, and footers. These help plan the structure before building the real website.

Page 4.1 — Home

The Home page includes a large hero image at the top, a strip with three key facts, three featured exhibition cards, a quick info section with hours and address, and a footer.

Page 4.2 — About

The About page has a dark header, followed by two columns. The left side has five paragraphs explaining the museum’s history and purpose. The right side has two images that stay in place while scrolling. At the bottom, there is a timeline showing four important years.

Page 4.3 — Exhibitions

The Exhibitions page has a dark header, three filter tabs (All, Permanent, Current), and six exhibition cards. Each card has an image, title, badge, and short description. Permanent exhibitions are free, and the current exhibition has a price. All images open in a lightbox.

Page 4.4 — Visit Us

The Visit Us page gives all the practical information visitors need before arriving. It includes a cream header, a pricing table, opening hours, a list of facilities, seven entry rules, and a dark transport section showing train, bus, and address details.

Page 4.5 — Contact

The Contact page has a dark header, two columns, and a footer. The left column shows the museum’s address, phone number, hours, media email, website link, and social links. The right column shows a Google Maps embed and public transport information. There is no contact form because all enquiries must go through the official museum website.

05 — Content

This section lists all the content that will appear on each page of the website. It includes text, images, links, facts, and interactive elements. This helps make sure every page has the correct information before building the real website.
